# Names

The proper names in Jewel Blitz Arena, gathered so a rename starts in one file. Behavior,
numbers, and rules stay in the owning spec (`cast.md`, `stages.md`, `items-and-economy.md`,
`modes.md`, `combat-and-match.md`). This list does not add names the specs do not already
use. Where a group is counted but not titled, that is noted.

## World

- Luster Ring
- Heart Diamond
- Crown Vein
- House Vitre
- Chairman Vitre
- Dullglass
- The Press
- Cutters (masked House Vitre crew on the Harvester)
- Vein Guard (Pressworks automatons)

Accents: Flame, Tide, Storm, Stone, Star, Ice.

## Reserved words

Jewel, Luster, Blitz, Blitzed, Flare, Surge, Carat, Gleam, Chit, Scrap, Dullglass, Dross,
Wisp, Rally, Squall.

## Bosses

Not playable.

**The Harvester** (Terraces). Moves: Patrol, Intake, Beam, Stomp, Spray, Buck. Breakables:
legs, head housing, tail controller. Timeout move: Blade Sweep.

**Chairman Vitre** (The Pressworks). Moves: Swing-and-grab, Pistons, Spew, Live Hands,
Overpress. Timeout move: Flood.

- Weightless Toll (Fulgur Cloister gong)

Stage weapons that never enter inventory: ceremonial staff, incense cannon, and the weapons
dropped by Vein Guard (mortar, frost-staff, mallet, net, flame).

## Modes and menus

Duel, Tournament, Custom, Expedition, Online, Profile.

Tilly's Bench rooms: Buy, Sell, Tinker, Talk, Ledger, Fitting Room, Facet Wheel.

Other screens and aids: Photo Mode, Sound Test, Pocket Items, Bench Notes, Dodge Assist,
Target Line, Auto-pickup, edge bubble.

Custom presets: Classic, Brawl, Pure, Boss Rush, Sparring.

Tournament option: Remix.

House Rules, in unlock order: Pocket Items, Jewel cap, Luster timer, Blitzed damage taken,
Blitzed attack, Rally seconds, Phase clock.

Basic Rules (always available): time limit, teams, item frequency, item switch, handicap,
stage hazards, Wisps, CPU level.

## Statuses

Stun, Dizzy, Knockdown, Pin, Freeze, Petrify, Slow, Blinded, Carry, grab.

## Economy pickups

- Copper gleam, Silver gleam, Gold gleam
- Purse, Coffer

Currency is Carat. A failed Tinker pays Scrap and a Chit.
