# Cast

Owner: combat designer, character art. Fourteen fighters (twelve base, two unlockable) and
two bosses. Each fighter has a base kit and a **Blitzed** form with a **Flare** (repeatable
special, no Luster cost) and two **Surges** (A: left shoulder or Attack+Jump, B: right
shoulder or Strong+Jump, 420 Luster each unless the kit declares otherwise). Damage and
control budgets are in `balance.md`.

The twelve base fighters cover the archetype spread of a four-player brawler:

| Archetype | Fighters |
| --- | --- |
| All-rounder | Rook Citrine, Hob |
| Speed | Sable, Teo Cruz |
| Heavy | Bulwark Oyo, La Cumbre |
| Ranged / zoner | Delphine, Ferro Dusk |
| Aerial | Wren Ashfall |
| Spacing duelist | Lune Varga |
| Trickster | Pip & Pepper |
| Oddball | Clank |

The two unlocks remix existing kits: Captain Citrine (Rook's grandmother, heavy-leaning
all-rounder) and Tilly Setter (Delphine-adjacent zoner).

## 1. World

The **Luster Ring** is a circle of floating realms kept aloft by shards shed from the **Heart
Diamond**. Three shards in hand and a fighter **Blitzes**: the jewel writes an aspect over
their body for as long as the **Luster** gauge lasts. Flame, tide, storm, stone, star, and
ice are those aspects. They are costume, stage, and crafting flavor. They are not a type
chart, and no element beats another.

**House Vitre**, under **Chairman Vitre**, has capped the **Crown Vein**, the only deposit
that sheds Blitz Jewels. The Pressworks grinds them into **Dullglass**, a dead concentrate
that lets Vitre stop the Ring's drift and own every route between the realms. The tone is a
bright adventure with a cartoon villain. Story is one card per stage and a per-fighter
ending.

The fighters are rivals who want the jewels for their own reasons. The ladder runs inward:
the outer realms (Citrine Bazaar, Fulgur Cloister, Sunken Strongroom), the quarry terraces
and the Harvester, House Vitre's holdings (Rime Cutworks or the Orrery), and the Pressworks.

Metal and glass belong to Ferro, Clank, and the Press. Delphine's accent is sound, which
Dullglass swallows. One fighter or one stage owns each accent so the elements read as a
cast, not as a coat of paint on every move.

## 2. Base fighters

Format: name — role. Kit summary. Blitzed form: Flare / Surge A / Surge B. Surge
numbers are listed in §5.

**Rook Citrine — all-rounder, the face of the game.**
Courier of the Luster Ring. Balanced run and jump, a clean three-hit string ending in a
roundhouse, a Strong that launches. Her kit teaches the game: every verb is average.
Blitzed form **Citrine Kestrel**, a jewel-winged glider harness. Flare: a boost-dash slash
that can chain twice. Surge A: **Thermal Dive**, rises then dives on the nearest opponent
with a shockwave. Surge B: **Windlass**, a spinning wing-blade sweep that pulls actors in
from 4 m.

**Sable — speed, teleport feints.**
Stage magician and escape artist. Fastest run, lowest Life, a string of rapid short hits, a
Strong that is a vanish-and-reappear behind the target (short range, 30 f cooldown).
Blitzed form **Hollow Cloak**, a shadow silhouette with a floating mask. Flare: a blink
strike that leaves an afterimage decoy that absorbs one hit (one decoy at a time). Surge
A: **Curtain Call**, a multi-blink barrage on one target. Surge B: **Smoke Vault**, the
arena lights drop to 30% for 4 s. Every fighter stays drawn as a player-color silhouette,
Sable is drawn only as her mask, and her hits during the effect inflict Dizzy (immunity
rules apply). The original "stage-wide blackout" made a shared screen unreadable for all
four players.

**Bulwark Oyo — heavy grappler.**
Beacon-warden of the Crown Vein terraces. Slowest run, highest Life, a two-hit string that ends in a slam, a Strong
that is a command grab from 1.5 m (12 f startup, whiffs are punishable). After a throw start
he can carry the held opponent for up to 60 f; the carried fighter can mash out up to 50%
sooner.
Blitzed form **Lantern Golem**, stone and brass with a lit lamp in the chest. Flare: a ground
pound that sends a shockwave along the floor. Surge A: **Beacon**, the lamp fires a slow
beam that knocks down everything in a line. Surge B: **Groundswell**, grabs the floor and
heaves a section upward, launching everyone on it.

**Lune Varga — spacing duelist.**
Duelist with a rapier. Mid speed, long thin hitboxes, a string of lunges, a Strong that is a
riposte: if she is struck during its startup, she counters instead. The riposte is the one
counter in the cast. It is not a block, because she still takes the hit's damage at 50%.
Blitzed form **Argent Knight**, plate and a greatsword of light. Flare: a lunge that
repositions through the target. Surge A: **Measure**, a line of thrusts across the arena.
Surge B: **Coronet**, a rising spiral slash that carries the target up and drops them.

**Teo Cruz — rhythm, speed.**
Capoeirista and bazaar hand. Fast, flowing strings that move him forward, a Strong that is a
cartwheel kick. His dodge window is 8 f instead of 6.
Blitzed form **Carnival Jaguar**, a dancer-beast with feathered pelt. Flare: a somersault
kick combo that changes direction on input. Surge A: **Roda**, a spinning kick circle that
hits everyone around him three times. Surge B: **Bateria**, a drum stomp that staggers all
grounded actors within 8 m for 60 f.

**Wren Ashfall — aerial, float, multi-jump.**
Mountain hang-glider courier. Triple jump, glide while holding Jump, light hits, a Strong
that is a diving kick. Low Life. Strong on Spirewalk, weak in Behind the Counter.
Blitzed form **Thunderhead**, storm-wings and a cloud trail. Flare: an air-dash that leaves a
static trail that stuns on contact. Surge A: **Downdraft**, slams every airborne actor
within 10 m to the ground. Surge B: **Updraft**, a column that launches everyone within 3 m
and leaves Wren airborne.

**Delphine — zoner, sound.**
Opera singer from the crystal theatre. Mid speed, short melee, a Strong that is a sonic
cone pushing actors back 3 m without damage (the cast's only displacement Strong; 0 damage,
exempt from the Strong band, `balance.md` §3.1). Float on held Jump (slow fall).
Blitzed form **Crystal Choir**, three mirrored singers in formation. Flare: a projectile note
that travels along the floor and bounces off walls once. Surge A: **Aria**, a sustained
cone that damages and pushes for 2 s. Surge B: **Crescendo**, every actor within 12 m is
pulled toward Delphine for 1 s, then a burst. Fighters can attack and dodge while they
slide. The pull is not hard control.

**Clank — oddball, variants.**
A salvage automaton Tilly built out of cullet and Press scrap. It kept walking after she
told it to stop. Mid speed, awkward
string with long startup but huge hitboxes, a Strong that fires a spring-arm grab at 3 m.
Carries two held items instead of one (the only kit that does): Attack uses the right-hand
item, and Strong uses the left-hand item when both hands are full. Clank's Surges have
**four variants each**, selected by held direction at input. That makes his the biggest
moveset in the cast, which is the reason he exists. Each variant has its own cost in §5.
Blitzed form **Clank Mk.II**, a tracked siege chassis. Flare: a shoulder battery that fires
three bolts. Surge A (**Toolbox**): neutral = mortar, forward = ram, up = drop weight, down =
mine field. Surge B (**Spare Parts**): neutral = magnet pull of all loose items within 6 m
(and strips held items), forward = flamethrower sweep, up = chaff that Blinds, down = repair.

**Ferro Dusk — gunner, mid-range.**
Railway marshal with a lever-action. Mid speed, a melee string with the rifle stock, a
Strong that is a shot (the base kit's only ranged normal; 60 damage, tagged `ranged_strong`,
exempt from the Strong band and the Power scale, 4 rounds, reloads over 90 f). See
`balance.md` §3.1.
Blitzed form **Ironhorse**, locomotive armor with a boiler back. Flare: a steam-piston punch
with a 2 m shockwave. Surge A: **Full Steam**, a charge across the arena that carries
anyone hit. Surge B: **Signal Fire**, one flare per opponent drops where they are standing
and explodes after 60 f.

**Pip & Pepper — trickster, swap.**
Twin acrobats who share a slot and one Life bar. Only one is active; Strong swaps them, with
a 90 f cooldown. The incoming twin vaults in from 2 m behind and has 10 f of invulnerability
on arrival. The held item passes to the incoming twin. Pip is fast with weak hits; Pepper is
slower with knockdowns.
Blitzed form **Riptide**, both twins fused into a surf-spirit on a wave. Flare: a wave dash
that carries loose items along with it. Surge A: **Undertow**, a floor wave that trips and
drags everyone 4 m. Surge B: **Breaker**, a wall of water that crashes down the arena's
longest axis.

**Hob — all-rounder, control.**
Mess cook who quit a House Vitre haul. Mid speed, a cleaver string, a Strong that is a pan
swing (80 damage) that reflects projectiles and thrown items (reflection, not block; beams
cannot be reflected).
Blitzed form **Boiler Crab**, a steam-crab chef with vat claws. Flare: a steam vent cone
that scalds and Slows. Surge A: **Boil Over**, a scalding pool 4 m across for 4 s. Surge
B: **Pincer**, a long-range claw grab that drags and crushes.

**La Cumbre — heavy striker.**
Luchador and volcanologist. Slow, high Life, a two-hit string into a lariat, a Strong that
is a leaping body splash (`otg`). Takes 10% less knockback.
Blitzed form **Magma Idol**, a basalt colossus with a glowing core. Flare: a lava-fist that
leaves a burning patch. Surge A: **Eruption**, a pillar of fire under each opponent after
a 60 f telegraph. Surge B: **Caldera**, a leap and slam that cracks the floor in a 5 m
radius.

## 3. Unlockable fighters

**Captain Citrine — Rook's grandmother, retired route captain.**
Unlock: clear Tournament with seven distinct fighters. A Rook remix with a saber: slower,
more Life, a string with reach, a Strong that is a hooked pull. She also appears at Tilly's
Bench after unlock and can hand over rare gifts (see items spec).
Blitzed form **High Brass**, convoy plate over a brass frame. Flare: a volley of three prism
shots. Surge A: **Grapple Line**, a line that pulls a target in, then a saber flurry.
Surge B: **Holdfast**, chains burst from the floor and Pin every actor within 3 m.

**Tilly Setter — the shopkeeper.**
Unlock: clear Tournament with eight distinct fighters. A Delphine-adjacent kit: floats on a
**kite** when Jump is held, fights with a setter's pick on a line (long, low damage, a Strong
that hooks an item or an actor from 4 m). Lowest Life tier.
Blitzed form **Bell Casing**, a brass bell suit with a lamp, the rig she uses to go down
into vaults. Flare: a cast-and-reel that hooks the nearest actor and pulls them in. Surge A:
**Invoice**, fires three homing
ledger-slips. It is **repeatable**: 180 Luster per volley instead of 420, so a full gauge pays
for several. Surge B: **Closing Time**, a bell toll that drops an empty crate on every
actor within 5 m. The crates hit and shatter, and they never contain items, so the Surge
cannot flood the loose-item cap.

## 4. Bosses (not playable)

**The Harvester.** A house-sized walking excavator crewed by masked House Vitre Cutters.
Moves, breakables, and routes are in `stages.md` §3.1. Design intent: a puzzle with two valid
solutions that both reward cooperation. One fighter holds the intake's attention while the
other breaks a leg; the steady route and the burst route trade risk for speed.

**Chairman Vitre.** Chairman of House Vitre. Fights from a mechanized chair fed by Dullglass; the Press is his weapon. Moves in `stages.md` §3.2. Design intent: an attrition fight
where food and cards are the survival items and where Blitz, suppressed through the
sorting line, lands exactly when the fight begins.

Changes from v0.1: Caldera was 400 in a 5 m radius, which was 40% of a fighter's Life as an
area move, and could take 1,200 off a lobby at once. Holdfast (then Moorings) pinned for 2 s,
which broke the hard-control cap. Invoice had no damage figure, and at its old cost it could
stack six volleys with no ceiling. It is now held to the same damage-per-Luster ratio as
every other Surge.

v0.3 budget fixes, all inside the same rules: Clank's mortar was 260 (ratio 0.62, under the
±10% band) and is 280. His mine field was 4 × 90 with no per-target rule, so one fighter
could eat 360, and the variant fit neither the area cap nor the damage-per-Luster rule. It
is now a utility variant: 70 damage, one mine per actor per cast, fourth mine spare. The
point is the zone. Hob's pool was 240, over the 220 area cap, and is 50 per second to a max
of 200. Clank's repair was a 250 heal (ratio 0.60) and is 280, which is how a heal is priced
(`balance.md` §3.3).

### 6.1 Intended matchup pressure

These are the counter-relationships the kits are built around. They are hypotheses for M1
playtests, not results.

- **Heavies (Bulwark, La Cumbre)** beat close-range speed kits that have to commit, and lose
  to zoners who never let them in. Delphine's displacement Strong is the hardest counter.
- **Zoners (Delphine, Ferro, Tilly)** lose to Hob, whose pan reflects their projectiles, and
  to Sable's vanish.
- **Speed (Sable, Teo)** beat zoners and lose to Bulwark's command grab and Lune's riposte.
- **Lune** punishes predictable attackers and is weak to throws, which the riposte cannot
  catch.
- **Clank** gets more out of item-heavy matches than anyone else. With Item frequency set
  to off he is the weakest kit, which is acceptable.
- On `fall_is_ko` extra arenas, aerial kits are favored on purpose. Those arenas are
  Custom-only and labeled with a "favors aerial" tag on the stage select.

## 7. Art and animation budget per fighter

Base model, Blitzed model (full costume/form swap for all but Tilly, whose Blitzed form is a
suit over the base), eight shared cosmetic attachment sockets (head ×2, arm ×2, waist ×1,
leg ×2, back ×1) for Fitting Room items, four color palettes (two unlocked by the fighter's
first Tournament clear), ~45 animations base, ~25 Blitzed, one ending card.

**Readability rules for art:** every fighter is distinguishable by silhouette alone at the
camera's maximum pull-back. Player color appears on a fixed body region (scarf, sash, or
lamp) plus the outline. Cosmetics may not exceed 15% of the fighter's bounding volume and may
not use the four player colors as their dominant hue.

**Element color, separate from player color.** Flame reads orange-red, tide reads deep teal,
storm reads violet-white, stone reads ochre, star reads pale gold, ice reads pale blue.
Those six hues are not the four player colors. Storm and Squall never use a full-screen white
flash (`architecture.md` §7). When teams are on, a team shape sits beside the player color
(`combat-and-match.md` §13). Color is never the only carrier of team or element.
